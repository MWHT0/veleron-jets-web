Questions

Charter, explained.

How quickly can a private flight be arranged?

Short-notice departures are often possible, though timing depends on aircraft positioning, crew availability, airport slots and handling. We will tell you honestly what is achievable for your request.

How is the right aircraft selected?

We consider the sector length, passenger numbers, luggage, runway requirements and cabin preference, then present suitable options with operator detail.

Can I make changes after booking?

Changes are frequently possible, but are subject to the operator’s terms, crew duty limits and airport availability. Any cost implications are confirmed before changes are made.

Can pets travel onboard?

Many operators accept pets in the cabin. Acceptance depends on the operator, aircraft and the entry requirements of the destination country.

What identification is required?

Valid passports or accepted identification are needed for all passengers, along with any visas or permits required for the route.

How much luggage can I bring?

Baggage capacity varies considerably between aircraft categories. Please share luggage details with your request so aircraft can be matched correctly.

Who operates the flights?

Flights are performed by appropriately approved third-party operators. We are clear about who is operating each flight and share operator details on request.
